# Nairabox: Scaling a Digital Lifestyle Platform

**Role:** Product Manager, Events (2017–2018) → Lead Product Manager (2018–2021) → Chief Operating Officer (2021–2022)
**Company:** Nairabox (JORG Technologies), Lagos, Nigeria
**Sectors:** Event & cinema ticketing, food delivery, fintech-adjacent payments

## Context

Nairabox is a Nigerian digital lifestyle platform functioning as a mobile wallet: users fund an account and spend it on event tickets, cinema tickets, food delivery, bills, and airtime. I joined as Product Manager for the Events line and, over five years, moved through Lead Product Manager into Chief Operating Officer, taking on broader responsibility for cross-functional teams, product strategy, and company operations as the platform matured.

*Note: This case study describes the scope and outcomes of the work. The underlying business plans, partnership agreements, and legal documents from this period are confidential and are not included here.*

## Problem

When I joined, event ticketing in Nigeria still leaned heavily on physical tickets: long entry lines, high forgery risk, manual sales reconciliation, and no real-time visibility into inventory or attendance. Event-goers had little reason to trust or prefer digital tickets over paper ones, and the company had no systematic way to convert its user base into recurring subscribers rather than one-off buyers.

## What I did

**As Product Manager, Events (2017–2018)**
- Implemented API-based QR code ticketing, replacing manual paper processes, which cut customer entry wait times from roughly 15 minutes to 20 seconds and cut ticket printing and distribution costs by 30%
- Applied human-centered design principles to the ticketing flow specifically to reduce fraud risk, a core weakness of the paper-based system
- Managed 85% of the company's premium events and curated 10+ exclusive events for Visa cardholders, directly supporting card acquisition and usage goals for the partner bank

**As Lead Product Manager (2018–2021)**
- Led the strategic roadmap for a Mastercard discount campaign, resulting in a partnership valued at over $130,000 and an 80% increase in customer engagement and adoption
- Owned the full product lifecycle from ideation to end-of-life across the platform's core products, improving product efficiency by 20% and cutting time-to-market by 15%
- Mentored junior product managers and cross-functional teams, improving project management success rates by 25%

**As Chief Operating Officer (2021–2022)**
- Oversaw day-to-day operations and cross-functional teams across the company's two main product lines
- Built the business case that enabled the CEO to refocus the division toward the enterprise market, contributing to a 7% increase in profits while scaling nationwide
- Initiated collaboration with government stakeholders to build an accreditation and validation system for a 60,000-capacity venue
- Directed a product roadmap that shipped 3 new products and 4 new integrations with top-tier Nigerian banks
- Coordinated development of two SaaS products for cinemas and movie distribution, contributing to 15%+ revenue growth and expanded market share
- Grew third-party API revenue by 50% within 9 months
